## Goals

## Approach and Implementation
I spent the first two days prompting AI models directly on candidate problems to get a feel for what kinds of results might realistic to potentially make progress on over the summer. On Wednesday I read up on recent AI-for-math results, like OpenAI's discrete geometry result and DeepMind's work on AlphaEvolve to understand the current state of the art in AI-assisted discovery.
 
Thursday I met with the grad student I will be working with for the rest of the summer, Javier, to discuss how we will work together. Friday I met with my mentor, Javier, and another professor we will be working with, Dr. Binghui Peng. The professors suggested that we spend the next few weeks reading papers in the group's areas and identifying open directions worth pursuing. I also started learning a little bit of Lean. Additionally, to keep track of all the different possible directions we could take, Dr. Peng suggested we create some way to track all the papers and work we do. So, I also looked into the various ways to track our work, settling upon a GitHub Pages / MkDocs research site, for which I created a repository.
